Dear Editors of The Electronic Journal of Combinatorics,

Please consider \emph{The minimum number of distinct abelian squares in a binary circular word}, by Deep Bhattacharjee.

The paper proves the sharp lower bound $\lfloor(n-1)/2\rfloor$ for every binary circular word of positive length and classifies all equality cases. It proves the odd-length part of Simpson's Conjecture~0.6 and corrects the even-length list by adding the alternating words when $n\equiv2\pmod4$, $n\ge6$. The argument is self-contained and proceeds through a double count of square witnesses and a classification of rigid words with a tight letter. The six principal conclusions are stated and proved in the accompanying Lean development.

An earlier preprint is available at \url{https://hexagonmath.org/2610.00473v1}. This edition uses the amsart layout and retains the proof. Code and verification information are available from \url{https://github.com/creelie/literate-octo-lamp}.
