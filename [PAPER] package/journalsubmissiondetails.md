# Journal submission details

Recommended mathematical fit: The Electronic Journal of Combinatorics.
Official instructions checked 26 October 2026: https://www.combinatorics.org/ojs/index.php/eljc/about/submissions
Portal: the journal's registration/login workflow on that page. No arXiv endorsement is required by the listed submission checklist.

The subject is extremal combinatorics on words: a sharp bound for every length and a complete classification, with an explicit correction to Simpson's even case. amsart is the requested preprint format. Initial submission is a PDF; the journal requires its own e-jc.sty for accepted final typesetting. This package does not claim that amsart is the journal's final house style.

Upload the manuscript PDF initially. Keep the portable TeX ZIP, verification record, cover letter and AI disclosure available as supporting files if requested. Existing preprint: https://hexagonmath.org/2610.00473v1. The replacement version has not yet been assigned. The new release and DOI receipt, when confirmed, are separate files in this package.

## Author checks before an actual journal submission
The journal requires that an AI-obtained proof be rewritten in the author's own words and that the author can vouch for every step. It also restricts submissions by authors who submitted another paper within the previous three calendar months, and requires confirmation of no concurrent journal consideration. The current Hexagon declaration remains “some parts” understood by a human; automated verification does not supply the journal's human attestation. These personal declarations have not been made on the author's behalf. Do not submit to the journal until they are satisfied.

The cover letter and AI disclosure PDF are prepared drafts, not signed attestations or official completed journal forms. No journal submission has been made, and acceptance is not guaranteed.
