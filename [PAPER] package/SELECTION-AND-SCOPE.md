# Paper 15 selection

Selected: The minimum number of distinct abelian squares in a binary circular word.
Existing record: https://hexagonmath.org/2610.00473v1
Existing repository: https://github.com/creelie/literate-octo-lamp

This is a complete general result on the circular-word minimum and all equality cases, with a corrected even-length classification. It has a self-contained written argument, matching general Lean statements, successful pinned repository CI, and fresh definition-level finite checks. Those distinguish it from finite-range results or papers with stated construction gaps.

The Gurvich-Naumova candidate was not chosen first after a search found the earlier September registry claim https://palomar-registry.org/entry?id=PALOMAR-2026-09-07-000013&version=1. That record needs substantive source/priority review before claims of first resolution. Its discovery is not a mathematical refutation of the local partition construction. The general three- and four-dimensional packing and geometry manuscripts require more extensive proof audits than were completed in this selection pass.

No exhaustive novelty claim or journal acceptance guarantee is made. The source conjecture is Simpson, arXiv:1802.04481v1, Conjecture 0.6. Existing Hexagon and repository records identify Deep Bhattacharjee as sole author; that is preserved.
